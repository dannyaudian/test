Executive Closing Message

Input once at the front. Connect every transaction document. Issue one receipt for every verified payment. Carry the same data seamlessly to the back.

FAST establishes one target operating model that connects:

Frontman Input→SPK→Booking Fee/DP→E-Receipt→Sales Order→Open Unit Bill→Settlement→Payment Allocation→AR Open→AFI→Billing→Delivery→Good Issue→STNK/BPKB

FAST ensures that:

  • Frontman and Administration inputs are mapped clearly.
  • The same data is not entered repeatedly.
  • Administration verifies rather than recopies — and that workload and SLA are recorded for KPIs.
  • SPK, SO, and billing numbers stay connected.
  • Every verified payment generates one e-receipt.
  • Customers can download the e-receipt.
  • AR Open is reduced only by active receipts.
  • Active payment requests reduce the Available Request Amount, but do not reduce AR yet.
  • Receipt cancellation restores the amount to AR and reevaluates the financial gate.
  • For credit/leasing, the required B2B DP appears in the Sales Order price structure (OTR, final value, required DP, remaining financed amount) — read-only, not typed by Frontman.
  • Each SPK has its own transaction trail in the mockup. Tabs and links do not open another SPK.
  • Cash billing unlocks at ≥30% funds received; leasing billing unlocks after full DP from B2B leasing.
  • Cash delivery unlocks after full payment; leasing delivery unlocks after the signed contract from B2B leasing.
  • Frontman downloads the leasing contract, uploads the signed contract, uploads the signed-contract photo, handover photo, and BSTKB. Frontman does not bill leasing.
  • Administration bills leasing paperlessly and issues receipts only after the Frontman package is complete and the full B2B DP has been paid. Missing documents return to Frontman as backflow, not a waiver gate.
  • Customers can open all unit invoices that are still open.
  • Customers can view status, blockers, next actions, and the order journey.
  • Approval Engine only handles failed compliance gates that are waivable.
  • Every decision and change is traceable.
