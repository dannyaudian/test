Preview skema · skenario document gate

Documents are uploaded once in SPK, stored in the vault, checked for validity, and then verified by Administration — not retyped in SO, AFI, or billing. A passed gate opens the booking fee invoice. Missing identity data is completed at the source (SPK). Expired but waivable items go to Approval Engine; Admin does not re-upload.
