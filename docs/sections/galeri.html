Scheme preview · approval gate scenarios

The Approval Engine only comes alive if a compliance gate fails and policy marks that rule as waivable. Frontman submits plus proof; Administration reviews; the decision owner decides. Discount, cashback, program, booking fee, 30% cash, paid in full, full leasing DP, and contract signature are not accepted in this queue.

Scheme preview · document gate scenarios
