Empat sudut pandang atas satu transaksi yang sama.

A. Frontman view

  • Current transaction stage.
  • Linked SPK, Sales Order, and billing numbers.
  • B2B leasing status per SO (submit through pelunasan, including backflow).
  • Required customer DP amount from B2B (read-only).
  • Download leasing contract; upload requested docs, signed contract, and billing evidence. Frontman does not send the leasing invoice.
  • Data already captured.
  • Missing information.
  • Input owner for missing data.
  • Document readiness.
  • Payment and receipt history.
  • AR Open per unit.
  • Active payment requests.
  • Available Request Amount.
  • Failed compliance rule.
  • Exception eligibility.
  • Exception decision status.
  • Billing readiness (including full DP from B2B).
  • Delivery eligibility (including TTD from B2B).
  • Good Issue.
  • STNK/BPKB.
  • Process hold reason.

B. Administration view

  • Transactions requiring verification, counted by type.
  • Paperless billing to leasing and leasing billing receipts (kuitansi) issued by Administration.
  • Required customer DP from B2B versus received.
  • B2B backflow waiting on Frontman.
  • Data requiring enrichment.
  • Duplicate-input warning.
  • Broken SPK–SO–Billing reference.
  • Missing receipt for a verified payment.
  • Unmatched payment.
  • Failed compliance rule.
  • Waivable versus non-waivable status.
  • Pending compliance exception decision.
  • Vehicle or document mismatch.
  • Aging and SLA clock per work item (start when the item enters the queue).
  • Queue volume — how many items, not an estimate.
  • Downstream impact of data correction.

C. Management view

  • Empat kursi: Kepala Administrasi (exception waivable), Kepala Cabang (STNK aging cabang), Area Business Head (volume/bottleneck area), Operation Manager (kirim & Good Issue).
  • Transaction volume by stage.
  • Bottleneck with drill-down to the same lineage.
  • Aging.
  • Locked transaction and cause (commercial gates remain non-waivable).
  • Approval inbox sesuai kursi — bukan 30%/lunas.

D. Customer View

Layar customer (shop.fast.id) adalah daftar pesanan, bukan katalog toko dan bukan workspace SAM. Jejak internal SPK→STNK tetap di Frontman/Administrasi. Customer klik pesanan untuk lacak progres, lalu memilih salah satu dari dua proses bayar yang terpisah — dengan AR Open, kuitansi, dan paket serah terima yang sama.

Daftar & lacak

  • List pesanan (Zenix berlangsung, Calya/aksesoris selesai).
  • Klik masuk untuk jejak: dipesan → DP → pelunasan → kirim → dokumen.
  • Tab Pesanan · Lacak · Bayar · Akun.
  • Calya FAST-01990 + aksesoris FAST-88102 hanya pelacakan.

Dua proses bayar (kondisional)

  • Penagihan Frontman — nominal dikunci (default Rp 100.000.000), QRIS/VA terkunci.
  • Pelunasan cashless — customer isi sendiri; maks. sisa di luar request (Rp 81.750.000) atau penuh AR Open (Rp 181.750.000) jika tidak ada request.
  • Skenario: ada keduanya / hanya request / hanya cashless / lunas (AR 0, menu bayar hilang).
  • Bukan satu bar CTA. EDC tidak ada di customer.

Bukti & kirim

  • E-kuitansi sebagai struk, nomor sama dengan cabang.
  • Pengiriman terkunci sampai cash lunas.
  • Paket bukti serah terima setelah unit diterima.
