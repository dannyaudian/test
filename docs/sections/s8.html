Four viewpoints of one and the same transaction.

A. Frontman view

  • Current transaction stage.
  • Linked SPK, Sales Order, and billing numbers.
  • B2B leasing status per SO (submit through settlement, including backflow).
  • Required customer DP amount from B2B (read-only).
  • Download leasing contract; upload requested docs, signed contract, and billing evidence. Frontman does not send the leasing invoice.
  • Data already captured.
  • Missing information.
  • Input owner for missing data.
  • Document readiness.
  • Payment and receipt history.
  • AR Open per unit.
  • Active payment requests.
  • Available Request Amount.
  • Failed compliance rule.
  • Exception eligibility.
  • Exception decision status.
  • Billing readiness (including full DP from B2B).
  • Delivery eligibility (including TTD from B2B).
  • Good Issue.
  • STNK/BPKB.
  • Process hold reason.

B. Administration view

  • Transactions requiring verification, counted by type.
  • Paperless billing to leasing and leasing billing receipts (receipt) issued by Administration.
  • Required customer DP from B2B versus received.
  • B2B backflow waiting on Frontman.
  • Data requiring enrichment.
  • Duplicate-input warning.
  • Broken SPK–SO–Billing reference.
  • Missing receipt for a verified payment.
  • Unmatched payment.
  • Failed compliance rule.
  • Waivable versus non-waivable status.
  • Pending compliance exception decision.
  • Vehicle or document mismatch.
  • Aging and SLA clock per work item (start when the item enters the queue).
  • Queue volume — how many items, not an estimate.
  • Downstream impact of data correction.

C. Management view

  • Four seats: Head of Administration (waivable exception), Branch Head (branch STNK aging), Area Business Head (area volume/bottleneck), Operation Manager (delivery & Good Issue).
  • Transaction volume by stage.
  • Bottleneck with drill-down to the same lineage.
  • Aging.
  • Locked transaction and cause (commercial gates remain non-waivable).
  • Approval inbox by seat — not 30%/paid in full.

D. Customer View

The customer screen (shop.fast.id) is an order list, not a store catalog and not a SAM workspace. The internal SPK→STNK trail stays within Frontman/Administration. Customers tap orders to track progress, then choose one of two separate payment flows — with the same AR Open, receipt, and handover package.

List & track

  • Order list (Zenix ongoing, Calya/accessories completed).
  • Tap in for the trail: ordered → DP → settlement → delivery → documents.
  • Tab Orders · Track · Pay · Account.
  • Calya FAST-01990 + accessories FAST-88102 are tracking-only.

Two payment processes (conditional)

  • Billing Request Frontman — nominal locked (default Rp 100.000.000), QRIS/VA locked.
  • Settlement cashless — customer fills it in themselves; max. the remainder outside the request (Rp 81.750.000) or the full AR Open (Rp 181.750.000) if there is no request.
  • Scenarios: both / request only / cashless only / paid in full (AR 0, pay menu disappears).
  • Not a single CTA bar. EDC is not available to the customer.

Proof & delivery

  • E-receipt as the receipt slip, same number as the branch.
  • Delivery locked until cash is paid in full.
  • Handover proof package after the unit is received.
