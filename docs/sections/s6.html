Sales Order — Booking Fee Gate

  • After Frontman menginput dan menyimpan SPK, sistem menagih booking fee ke customer.
  • Nominal booking fee berbeda-beda tergantung mobil/unit, ditarik dari master kendaraan.
  • Sales Order hanya dapat dibuat setelah minimum booking fee unit tersebut terverifikasi.
  • Rule ini non-waivable. Approval Engine tidak dapat membuka SO tanpa booking fee.

Billing Gate

  • Data STNK (name, address) sudah lengkap di SPK sebelum billing.
  • Billing dan AFI diajukan bersama. AFI sebelum billing, atau billing tanpa AFI, hanya lewat Approval Engine.
  • Cash: uang received minimal 30% dari nilai transactions (non-waivable).
  • Leasing: full DP sudah received dan e-PO leasing ada (total DP, nilai dibiayai, tenor). Angka DP dan e-PO ditarik dari B2B, bukan entered again di FAST.
  • Tanpa e-PO, paperless billing hanya lewat Approval Engine dengan decision owner Operation Manager. Full DP dan TTD tidak di-waive. Settlement leasing tetap butuh e-PO asli.
  • Billing tidak pending paid in full untuk cash, dan tidak memakai persentase 30% untuk leasing.
  • Rule 30% cash / full DP leasing bersifat non-waivable. Rule e-PO pada billing leasing bersifat waivable kepada Operation Manager.

Delivery Gate

  • Cash: transactions sudah paid in full.
  • Leasing: kontrak sudah ditandatangani customer. Status TTD ditarik dari B2B leasing setelah sales mengupload kembali kontrak yang sudah TTD (atau konfirmasi mitra).
  • AFI/DO, VIN, dan PDI tetap dicek sebagai kesiapan operasional.
  • Rule paid in full cash dan kontrak leasing bersifat non-waivable.

Sumber data kontrol

  • Minimum booking fee: master unit.
  • Persentase uang received cash: receipt aktif ÷ nilai transactions.
  • Full DP leasing: B2B leasing.
  • E-PO leasing (DP, nilai dibiayai, tenor): B2B leasing.
  • Kontrak TTD leasing: B2B leasing.
Central rules

No transaction moves forward unless the system confirms that the required data is complete, sourced from the correct owner, validated, and ready to be reused by the next process.

Approval Engine is activated only when a compliance rule fails and the policy classifies that rule as waivable.

Minimum booking fee untuk SO, ambang billing 30% cash / full DP leasing, serta requirement delivery paid in full cash / kontrak leasing TTD bersifat non-waivable. Billing leasing tanpa e-PO waivable kepada Operation Manager; settlement leasing tetap butuh e-PO asli.

Non-waivable legal, regulatory, and mandatory policy requirements cannot be overridden.
