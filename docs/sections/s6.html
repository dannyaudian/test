Sales Order — Booking Fee Gate

  • After the Frontman inputs and saves the SPK, the system bills the booking fee to the customer.
  • The booking fee nominal varies by car/unit and is pulled from the vehicle master.
  • A Sales Order can only be created after that unit's minimum booking fee is verified.
  • This rule is non-waivable. The Approval Engine cannot open an SO without the booking fee.

Billing Gate

  • STNK data (name, address) is already complete in the SPK before billing.
  • Billing and AFI are submitted together. AFI before billing, or billing without AFI, only through the Approval Engine.
  • Cash: at least 30% of the transaction value received (non-waivable).
  • Leasing: full DP already received and the leasing e-PO present (total DP, financed value, tenor). The DP figures and e-PO are pulled from B2B, not entered again in FAST.
  • Without an e-PO, paperless billing only through the Approval Engine with the Operation Manager as decision owner. Full DP and signature are not waived. Leasing settlement still requires the original e-PO.
  • Billing does not wait for paid in full for cash, and does not use the 30% percentage for leasing.
  • The 30% cash / full DP leasing rule is non-waivable. The e-PO rule on leasing billing is waivable to the Operation Manager.

Delivery Gate

  • Cash: the transaction is already paid in full.
  • Leasing: the contract is already signed by the customer. Signature status is pulled from B2B leasing after sales re-uploads the signed contract (or the partner confirms).
  • AFI/DO, VIN, and PDI are still checked as operational readiness.
  • The paid-in-full cash rule and the leasing contract rule are non-waivable.

Control data sources

  • Minimum booking fee: unit master.
  • Cash received percentage: active receipt ÷ transaction value.
  • Full DP leasing: B2B leasing.
  • Leasing e-PO (DP, financed value, tenor): B2B leasing.
  • Signed leasing contract: B2B leasing.
Central rules

No transaction moves forward unless the system confirms that the required data is complete, sourced from the correct owner, validated, and ready to be reused by the next process.

Approval Engine is activated only when a compliance rule fails and the policy classifies that rule as waivable.

The minimum booking fee for the SO, the 30% cash / full DP leasing billing threshold, and the paid-in-full cash / signed leasing contract delivery requirement are non-waivable. Leasing billing without an e-PO is waivable to the Operation Manager; leasing settlement still requires the original e-PO.

Non-waivable legal, regulatory, and mandatory policy requirements cannot be overridden.
