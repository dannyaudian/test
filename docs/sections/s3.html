KPI Items

The basis for KPIs is recorded work. Target figures (days, %, capacity) are agreed in the Target section after the baseline.

From guesswork to measurable

So far, Administration workload and timeliness have only been estimated — WhatsApp follow-ups, “the queue feels full,” and no SLA start time. In FAST, every Administration job enters a queue with type, transaction, and timestamp. Volume (how many) and aging/SLA (how long) can be measured. KPIs are built from that data, not estimates.

What can be counted — volume

Not an official target. These are the types of work the system already separates, so their counts can be turned into KPIs.

  • Queue verification (“Needs My Action”): how many transactions are pending Administration.
  • Split by type: Good Issue, unmatched payment, duplicate NIK, exception documents, internal classification, STNK aging.
  • Branch Ledger: volume of SPK, quotation, SO, AFI, DO, billing, B2B leasing, receipt, and payments per status.
  • Paperless billing requests to leasing, e-PO, and B2B document backflow. Billing without an e-PO goes to the Operation Manager — separate from DP/signed-contract waivers.
  • Waivable exceptions vs STNK escalation — separate from commercial gates (booking fee, 30%, paid in full, signed contract).

What can be measured — SLA / aging

Start time = when the job enters the queue. Duration is recorded per row. Policy thresholds (for example, how many hours counts as “on SLA”) are set later in Target, not invented here.

  • Aging per verification item (example in the mockup: hours, days — illustrative).
  • Time from Frontman submit → Admin check → Head of Administration decide, for waivable exceptions.
  • STNK aging after delivery (automatic escalation, not a paid-in-full waiver).
  • Time from unmatched payment until it is matched.
  • Good Issue time from proof upload until approved.

KPI families that can now be assembled

Official names and formulas will follow agreement. What changes: everything has a numerator and denominator from the system.

  • Branch workload — number of unlocked items per type, per Administration, per day.
  • Timeliness — the proportion of items completed within the agreed SLA hours; aging that breaches the threshold.
  • Queue quality — returned to the Frontman vs completed in Administration; corrected at source.
  • Capacity — items completed per person per period, without counting the Frontman's data as “work.”
