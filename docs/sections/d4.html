Deliverable 4 — Finance operations and compliance support

AR, receipts, billing/delivery gates, the B2B leasing workspace, and the Approval Engine all use the same data from the front. Finance does not bill from a separate spreadsheet.

Approval Engine boundary

The engine is only for a failed compliance gate and a waivable rule. Not for discount, cashback, program, booking fee, 30% cash, paid in full, full leasing DP, or contract signature.

Features

SPK–SO–billing and AR integrity

Every document carries the parent number. AR Open and Available Request Amount are computed by the system (see D2). Finance sees real-time exposure per unit, not overnight reconciliation.

Example in the mockup The receipt ledger #admin_kwt shows SPK/SO/method. Budi's lineage in #transactions.

Non-waivable financial gate

Cash: billing unlocked at ≥30% of funds received; delivery only when paid in full. Leasing: billing after full DP from B2B + e-PO; delivery after the signed contract from B2B. Only a missing e-PO for paperless is waivable to the Operation Manager.

Example in the mockup Commercial hold in #eskalasi — there is no decision button for fee, 30%, paid in full, DP, or signature.

Receipt reversal / reissue

Corrections are traced. Cancelled returns AR and re-locks the gate that was briefly unlocked. The related payment request becomes Cancelled or Requires Follow-up.

Example in the mockup The cancelled status stays visible in the branch ledger and customer history. The billing/delivery gate is re-evaluated.

Leasing workspace per Sales Order

B2B flow: submit → documents → contract → signature → DP → e-PO → paperless billing request → settlement. Illustrative partner: PT Danapura Multifinance. FAST is not the lessor. DP amount, tenor, and financed amount are read-only from B2B.

Example in the mockup Hiace SPK/26/CLD/00421 in #tx_hiace. Branch list: #admin_leasing.

Frontman vs Administration roles in leasing

Frontman: download the contract, upload after signing, photo of signature, photo of handover, BSTKB, documents requested by leasing. Does not bill leasing. Administration: paperless + leasing receipt only after the Frontman package is complete, full DP, and e-PO — or the OM approves paperless without an e-PO. Settlement still requires the original e-PO.

Example in the mockup Paperless exception: #exc_epo. Missing documents go back to Frontman as a backflow, not a DP/signature waiver.

Finance HO · B2B leasing book

Head Office has its own portal (teal chrome, ho.fast.id). The B2B leasing tab is the partner settlement book only: area ledger, SO dossier, unbilled, billed unpaid, partner paid / AR still open, and lead time. Clearing can fail for several reasons — customer underpayment is one of them. Partner PT Danapura Multifinance. FAST is not the lessor. HO does not bill and does not waive DP or signature.

Example in the mockup Left rail B2B leasing. A row click stays in the dossier; it does not open #tx_hiace or the Approvals desk.

Finance HO · last-seat Approvals

A separate HO desk from the leasing book. Sales type and leasing company start with Frontman on the same Sales Order, then Administration pack-check, then Head of Administration, Branch Head, ABH, and Operation Manager. Finance HO posts last. Also price adjustment and cancel billing after that chain. Not the Approval Engine. Not a DP, signature, 30%, or paid-in-full waiver. After a method change, cash 30% / paid in full and leasing DP / signature / e-PO stay non-waivable.

Example in the mockup Start on the SO: leasing → cash · cash → leasing · leasing company. Last seat: Approvals.

Exception routing

Frontman + proof → Administration verification → Head of Administration or Operation Manager per the rule. Conditional release, validity, scope. Non-waivable: complete the data or hold. STNK aging escalation to KC is operational, not the commercial engine.

Example in the mockup Accepted by the engine: name, address, AFI, e-PO. Not the engine: Sarah NPWP, Maria STNK, 30%/paid-in-full hold. Inbox: #mgmt_inbox.
