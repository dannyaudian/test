D2

Deliverable 2 — Cashless payments and e-receipts

Channels (QRIS, VA, CDM, Digiroom, EDC) are only ways to pay. What binds the transaction is the payment request, Banking API verification, and one receipt number per fully paid payment.

What changes

Payment confirmation, allocation, and receipts are not typed at the cashier. A request that is not yet paid does not issue a receipt and does not reduce AR Open. It is the active receipt that reduces AR.

Features

Booking fee: request locked

After the SPK + document gate, the system bills the unit master amount. The customer does not enter the amount themselves. EDC is only on the Frontman device. Without a fee receipt, the SO is not unlocked.

Example in the mockup Dewi Rp 3.000.000 in #customer_booking and #customer_booking_qris. Two ways, one request, one receipt KWT/26/CLD/009301.

Settlement: Frontman request or customer deposit

Process 1 — Frontman creates a request within the Available Request Amount limit; QRIS/VA locked. Process 2 — the customer opens the unit invoice from home and enters the amount; QRIS/VA is issued after the amount. These are two processes, two receipts — do not merge them.

Example in the mockup Process 1 Budi: #cashless (Frontman) and #tagihan_customer. Process 2: #digiroom — enter the amount first, then a new QR/VA.

AR Open vs Available Request Amount

AR Open = unit invoice value − total active receipts. Available Request Amount = AR Open − active requests that are not yet paid and not yet expired. Pending requests must not pile up into overpayment.

Example in the mockup Budi: active receipts 008731 Rp 5 million + 009115 Rp 300 million reduce AR. Illustrative balance Rp 181.75 million. Pending requests do not reduce AR.

One payment, one receipt

Banking API verifies → allocation to unit/SO → one KWT number → e-receipt PDF. The same number on the customer's phone and in the branch ledger. Not yet paid = no KWT yet.

Example in the mockup The customer downloads it at #e_kuitansi. The branch sees the same number at #admin_kwt.

Receipt status and corrections

Issued / Active / Reversed / Cancelled / Reissued. Not overwritten. Cancelled returns AR and re-evaluates the billing/delivery gate. The customer still sees the cancelled history.

Example in the mockup Status filter in the branch ledger. Cancelled stays as a history row, not deleted. The 30%/paid-in-full gate is re-evaluated after cancellation.

Open unit bill on the customer home

The customer sees every unit invoice still open, request status, and downloads the PDF. Frontman and customer use the same AR amount. There is no EDC in the customer's store.

Example in the mockup Order List #customer — Zenix still open, the already-delivered Calya is separate.

Flow in the mockup

View AR / request→Payment channel→Banking API→1 KWT number→AR decreases→Gate evaluated

What does not change

No verified payment, no receipt. Cash: billing ≥30% of funds received; delivery only when paid in full. Leasing: billing after full DP + e-PO (a missing e-PO is waivable to the OM for paperless); delivery after the signed contract from B2B.
