D2

Deliverable 2 — Cashless payments and e-receipts

Channels are only ways to pay. QRIS is capped at Rp 10.000.000 per barcode. A larger amount (for example Rp 50.000.000) is paid with several company QRIS — one Open receipt each. VA still pays the full request in one transfer. Administration controls every Open instrument.

What changes

Payment confirmation, allocation, and receipts are not typed at the cashier. QRIS max Rp 10.000.000 per barcode. To pay Rp 50.000.000 the customer scans five company QRIS. Each generate mints an Open KWT. Open does not reduce AR. Only Active after Banking API on that slice. VA remains the one-shot path for the full request.

Features

Booking fee: request locked

After the SPK + document gate, the system bills the unit master amount (within the QRIS cap). The customer does not enter the amount themselves. Frontman generates company QRIS → Open KWT. EDC is only on the Frontman device. Without an Active fee receipt, the SO is not unlocked.

Example in the mockup Dewi Rp 3.000.000 in #booking, #customer_booking, and #customer_booking_qris. Open then Active KWT/26/CLD/009301 on #admin_kwt.

Settlement: Frontman request or customer deposit

Process 1 — Frontman creates a settlement request within the Available Request Amount limit. Pay by VA once, or by QRIS in slices of max Rp 10.000.000 (Rp 50.000.000 = 5 barcodes). Process 2 — the customer enters an amount; VA once or several QRIS. Two processes, separate receipts.

Example in the mockup Budi: #cashless chip “Rp 50.000.000 · 5 barcodes”. Admin sees each Open slice on #admin_kwt.

AR Open vs Available Request Amount

AR Open = unit invoice value − total active receipts. Available Request Amount = AR Open − active requests that are not yet paid and not yet expired. Pending requests must not pile up into overpayment.

Example in the mockup Budi: active receipts 008731 Rp 5 million + 009115 Rp 300 million reduce AR. Illustrative balance Rp 181.75 million. Pending requests do not reduce AR.

One payment, one receipt

Company QRIS generate → Open KWT (one number per barcode, max Rp 10.000.000). Repeat until the target is met. Banking API on that QRIS id → Active. Personal/swapped QRIS does not match. Administration can void the Open slice; posted slices stay Active.

Example in the mockup Dewi booking one QRIS at #booking. Budi Rp 50 million = 5 slices at #cashless.

Receipt status and corrections

Open / Active / Reversed / Cancelled / Reissued. Open is the instrument after company QRIS generate. Not overwritten. Voiding Open does not return AR (AR never moved). Cancelling Active returns AR and re-evaluates the billing/delivery gate.

Example in the mockup Administration voids Open QRIS from the receipt book. Cancelled stays as a history row. The 30%/paid-in-full gate is re-evaluated only after an Active cancellation.

Open unit bill on the customer home

The customer sees every unit invoice still open, request status, and downloads the PDF. Frontman and customer use the same AR amount. There is no EDC in the customer's store.

Example in the mockup Order List #customer — Zenix still open, the already-delivered Calya is separate.

What does not change

No verified payment, no Active receipt and no AR movement. Open KWT is instrument control, not a cashier-typed proof. Cash: billing ≥30% of funds received; delivery only when paid in full. Leasing: billing after full DP + e-PO (a missing e-PO is waivable to the OM for paperless); delivery after the signed contract from B2B.
