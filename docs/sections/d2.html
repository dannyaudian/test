D2

Deliverable 2 — Cashless payments and e-receipts

Channels (QRIS, VA, CDM, Digiroom, EDC) are only ways to pay. Company QRIS is bound to the branch NMID. Generating it mints an Open receipt that Administration can control. Banking API on that instrument makes the receipt Active and then AR moves.

What changes

Payment confirmation, allocation, and receipts are not typed at the cashier. Generating company QRIS mints an Open receipt bound to the branch NMID so Administration can control the instrument. Open does not reduce AR Open. Only an Active receipt after Banking API reduces AR.

Features

Booking fee: request locked

After the SPK + document gate, the system bills the unit master amount. The customer does not enter the amount themselves. EDC is only on the Frontman device. Without a fee receipt, the SO is not unlocked.

Example in the mockup Dewi Rp 3.000.000 in #customer_booking and #customer_booking_qris. Two ways, one request, one receipt KWT/26/CLD/009301.

Settlement: Frontman request or customer deposit

Process 1 — Frontman creates a request within the Available Request Amount limit, then generates company QRIS (Open KWT). QRIS/VA locked. Process 2 — the customer opens the unit invoice from home and enters the amount; company QRIS/VA is issued after the amount. These are two processes, two receipts — do not merge them.

Example in the mockup Process 1 Budi: #cashless (Frontman) and #tagihan_customer. Process 2: #digiroom — enter the amount first, then a new QR/VA.

AR Open vs Available Request Amount

AR Open = unit invoice value − total active receipts. Available Request Amount = AR Open − active requests that are not yet paid and not yet expired. Pending requests must not pile up into overpayment.

Example in the mockup Budi: active receipts 008731 Rp 5 million + 009115 Rp 300 million reduce AR. Illustrative balance Rp 181.75 million. Pending requests do not reduce AR.

One payment, one receipt

Company QRIS generate → Open KWT (same number, not yet Active). Banking API verifies that QRIS id → allocation → Active → e-receipt PDF. A personal or swapped Frontman QRIS does not match the Open instrument; that money is Unmatched. Administration can void Open QRIS; Frontman must regenerate the company barcode.

Example in the mockup Budi: generate at #cashless → Open KWT/26/CLD/009220 on #admin_kwt and #admin_pay (Open QRIS filter). Customer PDF after Active at #e_kuitansi.

Receipt status and corrections

Open / Active / Reversed / Cancelled / Reissued. Open is the instrument after company QRIS generate. Not overwritten. Voiding Open does not return AR (AR never moved). Cancelling Active returns AR and re-evaluates the billing/delivery gate.

Example in the mockup Administration voids Open QRIS from the receipt book. Cancelled stays as a history row. The 30%/paid-in-full gate is re-evaluated only after an Active cancellation.

Open unit bill on the customer home

The customer sees every unit invoice still open, request status, and downloads the PDF. Frontman and customer use the same AR amount. There is no EDC in the customer's store.

Example in the mockup Order List #customer — Zenix still open, the already-delivered Calya is separate.

Flow in the mockup

View AR / request→Generate company QRIS→Open KWT→Banking API on that NMID→Active→AR decreases

What does not change

No verified payment, no Active receipt and no AR movement. Open KWT is instrument control, not a cashier-typed proof. Cash: billing ≥30% of funds received; delivery only when paid in full. Leasing: billing after full DP + e-PO (a missing e-PO is waivable to the OM for paperless); delivery after the signed contract from B2B.
