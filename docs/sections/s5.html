5

Concept & Details

Empat lapis konsep: design principle, straight-through data flow, standard data handling logic, dan customer journey per stage.

Layer 1

FAST Design Principle

Frontman-drivenInput-onceSystem-controlledSeamless front-to-backCompliance-secured
  • Frontman menginput data customer dan transaksi pada sumber awal.
  • Administrasi memverifikasi dan melengkapi data internal.
  • Sistem mencegah redundant input.
  • Data valid digunakan kembali.
  • Nomor SPK, SO, billing, payment reference, dan kuitansi terhubung.
  • Setiap pembayaran terverifikasi menghasilkan e-kuitansi.
  • Data dibawa dari SPK sampai STNK/BPKB.
  • Failed compliance rule yang waivable diarahkan kepada decision owner.
  • Seluruh perubahan memiliki audit trail.
Core statement

The Frontman captures customer and transaction data once. Administration verifies and enriches only where required. The system carries the same validated data and document references from SPK through Sales Order, billing, payment, receipt, AFI/DO, delivery, Good Issue, and document completion without re-entry.

Layer 3

Standard Data Handling Logic

Step 1 — Capture at source

Data dimasukkan oleh pihak yang memiliki sumber informasi paling valid.

Step 2 — Validate

Sistem memeriksa format, kelengkapan, duplikasi, master data, dan reference integrity.

Step 3 — Verify or enrich

Administrasi memverifikasi data dan hanya menambahkan data internal yang belum tersedia.

Step 4 — Store as source of truth

Data valid disimpan sebagai data transaksi resmi.

Step 5 — Reuse downstream

Data digunakan otomatis oleh proses berikutnya tanpa input ulang.

Step 6 — Correct at source

Apabila ada kesalahan, koreksi dilakukan pada data asal dan bukan melalui input berbeda pada proses belakang.

Step 7 — Control changes

Perubahan setelah transaction lock atau compliance exception decision harus menggunakan workflow, version control, dan audit trail.

Layer 4

End-to-End Customer Journey

SPK No.→Booking Fee/DP + Receipt→SO No.→Settlement + Receipt(s)→Billing No.→AFI/DO→Delivery→Good Issue→STNK/BPKB

Setiap stage memiliki: Frontman action, Administration action, system-generated data, data carried from previous stage, new data genuinely required, system validation, compliance gate, dan output yang digunakan stage berikutnya.

Stage 1

SPK

Frontman inputs

  • Customer identity.
  • Customer contact.
  • Alamat pemesan.
  • Nama dan alamat STNK, termasuk kabupaten/kota.
  • Purchase method (tunai atau leasing).
  • Unit type and colour.
  • Accessories.
  • Customer documents for SPK entry.
  • Documents for later STNK/BPKB filing.

Administration role

  • Verify customer data.
  • Verify documents.
  • Identify missing or invalid requirements.
  • Add internal classification if required.

System generates

  • Customer ID.
  • SPK number.
  • Area PO from kabupaten/kota on the STNK address.
  • OTR price from that PO area.
  • Repeat-order status.
  • Document readiness.
  • Minimum booking fee according to the selected unit.

Data carried forward

Customer, transaction, unit, document, dan compliance data digunakan oleh booking fee/DP request, Sales Order, billing, AFI/DO, delivery, serta STNK/BPKB.

Business rule

Customer and transaction data captured at SPK must not be re-entered at subsequent stages. Unit OTR follows the PO area generated from kabupaten/kota on the STNK address. Booking fee is billed only after this SPK data is saved.

Stage 2

Booking Fee/DP and Sales Order

Frontman action

  • Simpan SPK.
  • Monitor tagihan booking fee yang dikirim sistem ke customer.
  • Confirm payment instruction bila perlu dikirim ulang.
  • Submit Sales Order request setelah minimum booking fee unit terverifikasi.

Administration role

  • Handle unmatched or exceptional payment only.
  • Tidak memasukkan ulang payment status.
  • Tidak menentukan nominal booking fee secara manual.

System process

  • Ambil minimum booking fee dari master unit.
  • Tagihkan booking fee ke customer segera setelah SPK tersimpan.
  • Generate payment reference.
  • Verify payment.
  • Match payment.
  • Generate a unique receipt.
  • Link receipt to SPK.
  • Bandingkan nominal masuk dengan minimum unit.
  • Convert the saved SPK into a Sales Order only when the booking fee meets the unit minimum.
  • Carry pemesan, STNK, unit, PO-area price, accessories, and documents without re-entry.
  • Generate SO number with originating SPK reference.
  • Add SO reference automatically to receipt lineage.

Data carried forward

  • Customer ID.
  • SPK number.
  • Sales Order number.
  • Unit data.
  • Transaction structure.
  • Booking fee/DP amount.
  • Payment reference.
  • Receipt number.
  • Payment verification.
Business rule

Setelah SPK tersimpan, sistem menagih booking fee ke customer. Nominal minimum mengikuti unit yang dipilih. Tidak ada booking fee terverifikasi sesuai minimum unit, tidak ada Sales Order. Ketika booking fee meet, data SPK dikonversi menjadi Sales Order — bukan diisi ulang. Kuitansi tertaut ke SPK, lalu nomor SO ditambahkan pada lineage.

Stage 3

Settlement Payment and E-Receipt

Frontman action

  • View AR Open per unit atau Sales Order.
  • View active payment requests.
  • Create settlement payment request within Available Request Amount.
  • Resend, adjust, or cancel an active request.
  • Monitor partial or full payment.
  • View accumulated active receipts.
  • View outstanding balance.

Administration role

  • Resolve unmatched payment.
  • Handle documented exception.
  • Tidak menghitung ulang outstanding secara manual.

System process

  • Calculate AR Open from active receipts.
  • Calculate Available Request Amount after deducting active payment requests.
  • Validate request nominal against real-time availability.
  • Expose open unit bills on Customer Home.
  • Record every verified payment.
  • Allocate payment.
  • Generate one unique receipt for each verified payment.
  • Reduce AR by the active receipt amount.
  • Link every receipt to the payment reference, SPK, SO, and billing document when available.
  • Deliver the digital receipt to the customer.
  • Close or adjust overlapping payment requests.
  • On receipt cancellation, restore AR and reclassify the linked request.
  • Calculate accumulated active receipts.
  • Determine Partially Paid atau Fully Paid status.

Data carried forward

  • Payment and receipt history.
  • Billing readiness.
  • Account Receivable monitoring.
  • Delivery eligibility.
  • Management dashboard.
  • Customer notification.
Business rule

Every verified payment is recorded once, assigned one unique receipt number, and deducts AR by the active receipt amount. Pending requests do not reduce AR. If a receipt is cancelled, AR is restored, the linked request is reclassified, and all dependent financial gates are recalculated.

Stage 4

Billing

Frontman action

  • Submit billing-readiness request.
  • View every failed compliance rule.
  • Complete missing requirements.
  • Submit compliance exception only when the failed rule is marked waivable.

Administration and Finance role

  • Verify compliance evidence.
  • Confirm whether the failed requirement has been completed.
  • Review eligible compliance exception without changing transaction data.
  • Tidak memasukkan kembali customer, unit, payment, atau Sales Order data.

System pulls automatically

  • Customer data.
  • SPK number.
  • Sales Order number.
  • Unit information.
  • Final transaction value from source system.
  • Purchase method.
  • Booking fee according to unit.
  • Payment and receipt history.
  • Funds-in percentage.
  • Full DP required and received from B2B leasing (shown on the Sales Order pricing structure when payment type is credit/leasing).
  • Leasing e-PO (customer total DP, financed amount, tenor) from B2B.
  • Settlement status.
  • Outstanding balance.
  • Document readiness.
  • Compliance-gate results.
  • Active exception decision, conditions, and expiry.

System generates

  • Billing readiness.
  • Billing Locked atau Billing Ready status.
  • Failed-rule and lock reason.
  • Exception eligibility and decision route when waivable.
  • Billing document number after the gate is passed or a valid exception permits conditional release.
  • Permanent linkage between billing number, Sales Order number, and originating SPK number.
  • Automatic enrichment of related receipt lineage with the billing number.
Business rule

Billing uses validated upstream data and must not require transaction re-entry. Untuk cash, billing hanya terbuka apabila uang masuk sudah minimal 30% dari nilai transaksi. Untuk leasing, billing hanya terbuka apabila full DP sudah diterima dan e-PO leasing ada (total DP, nilai dibiayai, tenor); keduanya ditarik dari B2B. Tanpa e-PO, paperless hanya lewat Operation Manager. Referensi SPK dan SO harus konsisten. Syarat 30% cash / full DP leasing bersifat non-waivable.

Stage 5

AFI/DO

Administration inputs

Hanya data administratif baru yang belum tersedia pada proses sebelumnya.

System pulls automatically

  • Customer identity.
  • STNK name.
  • Customer address.
  • Supporting documents.
  • Sales Order.
  • Unit type.
  • Color.
  • VIN.
  • OPO/OPK.
  • Billing status.

System process

  • Auto-populate AFI.
  • Auto-populate DO.
  • Match customer and vehicle data.
  • Identify mismatch.
  • Lock only affected SO.
Business rule

No manual re-entry of validated customer, STNK, Sales Order, or vehicle data.

Stage 6

Delivery

Frontman inputs

  • Delivery request.
  • Delivery schedule.
  • Delivery location.
  • Recipient information.
  • Customer confirmation.

Administration role

  • Verify operational readiness.
  • Handle delivery exception.
  • Tidak memasukkan kembali financial status.

System pulls automatically

  • Customer data.
  • Unit and VIN.
  • Billing status.
  • AFI/DO status.
  • Purchase method.
  • Payment history.
  • Outstanding balance / lunas status for cash.
  • Leasing contract signed status from B2B leasing.
  • AR status.
  • PDI status.
  • Delivery authorization.

System generates

  • Eligible for Delivery.
  • Locked for Delivery.
  • Hold reason.
  • Required action.
Business rule

Delivery eligibility memakai data upstream yang sama. Untuk cash, delivery hanya terbuka setelah transaksi lunas. Untuk leasing, delivery hanya terbuka setelah kontrak sudah ditandatangani customer; status TTD ditarik dari B2B leasing. Syarat delivery ini bersifat non-waivable.

Stage 7

Good Issue

Frontman inputs

  • BSTKB.
  • Customer signature.
  • Vehicle receipt photo.
  • VIN / identity photo.
  • Timestamp and geotag.
  • Surat Kuasa when applicable.

System pulls automatically

  • Customer.
  • Sales Order.
  • Unit.
  • VIN.
  • Recipient from SPK.
  • Delivery authorization.
  • Financial clearance.
  • AFI/DO.
  • PDI status.

System process

  • Validate evidence completeness.
  • Match delivery with SO and VIN.
  • Record Good Issue.
  • Publish the same evidence pack to the customer account.
  • Close delivery milestone.
Business rule

Good Issue must use existing customer, unit, and delivery data without re-entry. The evidence pack is the authentic record — not dealer CCTV. Cash still cannot be handed over before lunas.

Stage 8

STNK/BPKB

Administration inputs

Hanya informasi baru terkait proses dokumen kendaraan dan penyelesaiannya.

System pulls automatically

  • Customer identity.
  • STNK name.
  • Customer address.
  • Unit.
  • VIN.
  • Sales Order.
  • AFI/DO.
  • Delivery date.
  • Supporting documents.

System process

  • Track milestones.
  • Detect aging.
  • Send escalation.
  • Notify Frontman and customer.
  • Close completed document milestones.
Business rule

Customer and vehicle data validated at the front must continue through document completion without being typed again.
